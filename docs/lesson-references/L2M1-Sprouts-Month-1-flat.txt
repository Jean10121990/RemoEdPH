Week 4 Lessons 16–20/22: Dedicated Review, Remediation, and Gamified Integration weeks, where kids practice what they learned in the live lessons and complete their virtual garden milestones in the AI app. Sample 22-Lesson Roadmap (Level 1 Month 1) Week 1: Focus on Letter A, the Color Red, and the Sun. This week introduces environmental stewardship by learning to say hello to trees, paired with the color blue. Week 3: Focus on Letter C, the Color Yellow, and Integration. This week completes the phonetic triplet and introduces the final primary color, linking the yellow sun back to God the Creator.
