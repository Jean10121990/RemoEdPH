REMOED PRIVACY POLICY

Platform: RemoEd (Filipino ESL & Education Platform)

Effective Date: ________________________

RemoEd is committed to protecting the privacy and security of our freelance tutors, students, and community members. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Teacher Data: Legal name, contact details, payment information, government identification, educational background, and performance metrics.
  • Profile Nicknames: Display names used across public profiles and virtual classrooms to safeguard teacher identity.
  • Student Data: Student names, age, learning assessments, and attendance records (collected under parental consent).

2. How Information is Used

  • Managing independent contractor payouts, incentive calculations, and performance tracking.
  • Facilitating 1-on-1 virtual instruction and educational progress reports.
  • Protecting teacher and child safety through authorized platform aliases (nicknames).
  • Coordinating voluntary company community activities (tree planting, feeding programs).

3. Safeguarding Child & Student Information

  • Child Safety First: RemoEd serves young learners. Tutors are strictly prohibited from storing, capturing, or sharing student names, photos, video clips, or personal details on private devices or social media platforms.
  • Non-Disclosure: Student records are confidential and accessible solely through the secure RemoEd system.

4. Data Security & Storage

  • All electronic agreements, signatures, and personal records are encrypted and stored in secure database environments.
  • Access to sensitive personal files is strictly limited to authorized RemoEd administrative officers.

5. Consent & Electronic Signatures

By selecting "I Accept" or utilizing the platform, you consent to the collection and processing of data as described in this Privacy Policy.
