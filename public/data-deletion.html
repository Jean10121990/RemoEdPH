User data deletion

How to ask RemoEdPH to delete your account and personal data, including information received from Facebook Login or Google sign-in.

Last updated: October 2026

Request deletion

Email support@remoedph.com from the email address on your RemoEdPH account.

  • Subject: Delete my RemoEdPH account
  • Your RemoEdPH username, if you know it
  • The email used to sign in (including Facebook or Google, if that is how you registered)
  • Whether you want the student account, the teacher account, or both deleted

A parent or guardian should send the request for a student account. We may ask you to confirm you control that email before we delete anything.

What we delete

After we confirm the request, we delete or anonymize the account profile, sign-in links (including Facebook and Google), and other personal data we no longer need to provide the service.

We may keep limited records when the law, payment partners, or safety reviews require it (for example invoices, fraud prevention, or an open incident). Those records are not used for marketing.

How long it takes

We aim to complete confirmed deletion requests within 30 days and will email you when it is done.

See also the Privacy Policy.
