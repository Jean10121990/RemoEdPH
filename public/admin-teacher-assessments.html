RemoEd Teacher Skills Assessment Rubric 1 (Skills)

Rate each skills category 1–5 during observation or testing. Add short evidence notes (e.g., “Pronunciation clear in minimal pairs exercise”).

Category Criteria Rating Scale (1–5) Notes / Examples
Speaking Fluency, clarity, models natural conversation 1 = Hesitant, 5 = Fluent & confident Check pace, intonation, coherence
Reading Accuracy, comprehension, reading strategies 1 = Struggles, 5 = Excellent comprehension Observe read-aloud + silent comprehension
Typing/Writing Speed, accuracy, grammar in typed responses 1 = Frequent errors, 5 = Fast & accurate Useful for online teaching platforms
Pronunciation Sounds, stress, intonation, clarity 1 = Hard to understand, 5 = Clear & natural Compare with standard English phonology
Grammar Accuracy; ability to explain rules 1 = Frequent mistakes, 5 = Consistently accurate Assess spoken & written grammar
Vocabulary Range Breadth; can simplify/expand for learners 1 = Limited, 5 = Wide & flexible Check adaptability to student level
Listening Comprehension Understands student speech/questions 1 = Often confused, 5 = Always understands Key for interactive teaching

RemoEd Teacher Personality Assessment Rubric 2 (Personality)

Rate each personality category 1–5 based on observation and interaction.

Category Criteria Rating Scale (1–5) Notes / Examples
Interpersonal Communication Clarity, encouragement, rapport 1 = Weak, 5 = Strong & supportive Student feedback helps here
Professionalism Reliability, punctuality, responsiveness 1 = Unreliable, 5 = Highly professional Peer/admin evaluation
Cultural Awareness Sensitivity to diverse backgrounds; inclusive language/examples 1 = Insensitive/rigid, 5 = Consistently inclusive Adjusts content to culture; avoids bias
Technology Use Effective use of online tools/platforms 1 = Struggles with tools, 5 = Efficient & resourceful Uses LMS, breakout rooms, annotations smoothly
Student Engagement Motivates learners; interactive tasks 1 = Passive sessions, 5 = Highly interactive Checks for participation, uses prompts/games
36–45 = Advanced 26–35 = Competent 16–25 = Developing Below 16 = Needs Improvement
