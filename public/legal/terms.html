Terms of use

For parents and students using RemoEdPH classes.

RemoEd PH is a Philippine-based live 1-on-1 online ESL tutorial service in Cabanatuan City, Nueva Ecija. A parent or guardian agrees to these terms when creating an account, booking a class, or buying a plan. The full text is the Terms of Service.

Lessons

Each class is a 25-minute live lesson with a real teacher. It is a rendered tutorial service, not a download and not a physical product. A free assessment and trial classes come before a paid package. A completed lesson is non-refundable.

Cancel, credits, and refunds

Cancel or reschedule free at least 24 hours before class time in Philippine Standard Time (UTC+8). A later cancel or a no-show uses the credit. A tutor absence or a verified platform failure restores the credit or moves the class at no charge. Credits last for the plan window: 1, 3, 6, or 12 months. One 3-month extension is available after a parent progress check-in call. For a purchase governed by Korean law, a parent may request a refund within 7 days of purchase: full if no lesson was used, otherwise pro-rata for unused credits. Outside that window, a refund is only for repeated, documented RemoEd PH failures that rescheduling cannot resolve, pro-rata for unused credits.

Classes and recordings

Live classes are recorded for safety and quality assurance, as described in the Student Privacy and Recording Policy. Privacy questions go to privacy@remoedph.com.

Plans

Paid plans add lesson credits. Prices are confirmed at PayMongo checkout.

Safety

A teacher may pause a class and alert RemoEd if there is a technical failure, an unaccompanied young child, or a safety concern. RemoEd staff may join to observe or end that session.
