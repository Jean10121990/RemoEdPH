Student Privacy and Recording Policy

Version 2026-11-20. A parent or guardian accepts this policy for the child.

RemoEd PH, of Cabanatuan City, Nueva Ecija, Philippines, teaches children in live 1-on-1 English classes. RemoEd PH is the personal information controller under Republic Act No. 10173. This policy sits alongside the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.

Who accepts

Only a parent or guardian creates the account and accepts this policy. That consent covers the child, including children under 16 in Japan and Vietnam, under 14 in South Korea, and under 10 in Thailand.

Recording

A 25-minute live class may be recorded for safety and quality assurance. Authorized quality-assurance staff in the Philippines may review it. The recording is deleted after 7 days. If that class has an open safety or dispute ticket, the recording stays until the ticket is closed, and is then deleted. Recordings are not a public broadcast.

What we store

  • Parent and child details needed to run the class.
  • Booking, lesson, and progress records.
  • The date, policy version, and network address of each privacy acceptance.
  • The parent’s separate consent to processing the child’s information for live lessons.

Your rights

A parent may ask for access, correction, deletion, objection, or a portable copy. Write to privacy@remoedph.com or use Request child data deletion in the student profile. The steps are also on the data deletion page.

Marketing is separate

Using anonymized learning quotes or media in an educational showcase is optional. Declining that choice does not block the account or the class.

Families outside the Philippines

RemoEd PH keeps these accounts in the Philippines under the Data Privacy Act, including for families in Japan, South Korea, Thailand, and Vietnam.
