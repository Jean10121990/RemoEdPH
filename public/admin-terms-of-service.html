REMOED ADMIN & BACK-OFFICE TERMS OF SERVICE AND AGREEMENT

Company: RemoEd (Filipino ESL & Online Education Startup)

Effective Date: ________________________

By clicking "I Accept", signing electronically, or executing this document, you (“Admin”, “Staff Member”, or “Back-Office Personnel”) agree to comply with all terms outlined below, alongside the RemoEd Culture & Conduct Manual.

1. Operational Scope & Nature of Relationship

  • Administrative & Back-Office Support: The Staff Member is engaged to manage operational systems, administrative support, HR functions, marketing, or curriculum operations. Administrative staff serve as the bridge between platform vision and daily operations.
  • Classification Status: Engaged as an administrative contractor or core team member based on designated contract scope. Benefits eligibility (including SSS, Pag-IBIG, PhilHealth, or HMO) applies upon formal regular employment status.
  • Tax Responsibilities: Independent personnel are independently responsible for filing, reporting, and paying all applicable local and national taxes (including BIR filings) derived from earnings on the platform.

2. Elevated System Access & Privileged Responsibilities

  • Data Governance: Admins are entrusted with elevated system access to student accounts, teacher records, financial ledgers, and operational backend tools.
  • Integrity of Records: Admins must never tamper with, modify, or falsify platform records, payment logs, user ratings, or lesson schedules.
  • Account Credentials: Multi-factor authentication and strict credential security must be maintained at all times. Sharing admin account access with third parties is strictly prohibited.

3. Christian Niche, Values & Administrative Conduct

  • Mission & Standards: Built on a Christian worldview, RemoEd requires administrative staff to embody integrity, fairness, humility, and grace in all platform communications and system management.
  • Biblical Model of Gender & Harmony: All administrative staff uphold the biblical distinction of gender (male and female) across platform settings, content organization, and internal communications.
  • Pillars of Conduct: Staff members must model RemoEd's core cultural pillars: Joyful Learning support, Respect & Care, Integrity & Accountability, Collaboration, and Growth Mindset.

4. Compensation & Payout Schedule

  • Calculation: Compensation is calculated based on the agreed monthly retainer, hourly administrative rate, or project-based scope.
  • Payout Schedule: Distributed bi-monthly on the 15th and 30th/31st (or final day) of each month.
  • Adjustments: Payouts are subject to system auditing, performance incentives, or deductions for unauthorized policy violations.

5. Intellectual Property, Data Governance & Confidentiality

  • Proprietary Assets: Platform software, administrative tools, marketing materials, graphics, curriculum files, student databases, and internal operational methods (RemoEdPH / RemoEd) are exclusive intellectual property.
  • Strict Non-Disclosure: Admins must execute and abide by the Non-Disclosure Covenant (Annex C), maintaining strict confidentiality over internal financial metrics, user data, and strategic plans.

6. Corporate Culture & Community Stewardship

  • Community Engagement: Staff members actively participate and lead by example in company environmental and social outreach initiatives, including tree-planting programs, feeding projects, and community development.
  • Path to Career Growth: Opportunities for regular employment, added health benefits, and organizational leadership are evaluated based on system accuracy, performance indicators, and alignment with RemoEd culture.
