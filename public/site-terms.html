4. Refund, cancellation, and credits

  • A completed 25-minute live lesson is a fully rendered service and is non-refundable.

  • One 3-month extension is available when lessons remain. A parent requests it, and a teacher grants it after a short online progress check-in call with the parent. The extension is once per current validity window.
