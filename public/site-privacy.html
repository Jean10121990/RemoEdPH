Privacy Policy

How RemoEdPH collects, uses, and protects information for parents and students.

Last updated: September 2026

RemoEdPH respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you use our website, student portal, and live classrooms, and how we use that information.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: parent/guardian name, email, username, and password (stored securely).
  • Student details: child’s name or nickname, age/level information, and learning progress.
  • Booking & payment data: class schedules, plan purchases, and payment references from our payment partner.
  • Technical data: device/browser information and basic usage logs needed to run and secure the service.
  • Communications: messages you send to support or through in-platform channels.

2. How we use information

  • To create accounts, deliver classes, and manage bookings and credits.
  • To process payments and send receipts or service notices.
  • To improve teaching quality, curriculum placement, and platform reliability.
  • To respond to support requests and protect against fraud or abuse.

3. Children’s privacy

RemoEdPH serves young learners. Parent/guardian consent is required for enrollment. Teachers and staff must not share student photos, recordings, or personal details outside authorized RemoEdPH tools. Classroom recordings (when enabled for quality assurance) are handled under internal access controls and retention rules.

4. Sharing

  • We share information with teachers only as needed to conduct booked classes.
  • Payment processors receive data required to complete checkout.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect safety and platform integrity.

5. Storage & security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal data. No online system is perfectly secure; please use a strong unique password and keep it private.

6. Your choices

  • You may update account profile details in the student portal where available.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data by contacting support, subject to legal and operational requirements (for example, payment records).

7. Cookies & local storage

We use cookies or similar storage for login sessions, language preference, and basic site functionality. You can control cookies through your browser settings; some features may not work if they are disabled.

8. Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: support@remoedph.com

Teachers and contractors have a separate privacy acknowledgment inside the teacher portal. This page is for parents and students only.
