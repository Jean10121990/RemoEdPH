Privacy Policy

How RemoEdPH collects, uses, and protects information for parents and students.

Last updated: November 2026

English is the official text of this policy. Korean, Japanese, Thai, and Vietnamese translations are not published yet.

RemoEd PH, of Cabanatuan City, Nueva Ecija, Philippines, is the personal information controller for parent and student accounts under Republic Act No. 10173 (the Data Privacy Act of 2012). This policy explains what we collect for live 1-on-1 English lessons and how a parent can use their rights.

1. Who may consent

Only a parent or guardian creates an account and gives consent. That consent covers the child, including children under 16 in Japan and Vietnam, under 14 in South Korea, and under 10 in Thailand. The child does not create the account.

2. Information we collect

  • Parent account: name, email, username, and a password stored in hashed form.
  • Child details: name or nickname, age or level, and learning progress, given by the parent.
  • Bookings and payments: class times, plan purchases, and payment references from PayMongo.
  • Lesson recordings: the 25-minute live class, kept for quality assurance as described below.
  • Technical data: device and browser details and basic logs needed to run and secure the service.
  • Consent records: the policy version, the time, and the network address of each acceptance.

3. How we use information

  • To create the parent account, deliver live classes, and manage bookings and credits.
  • To process payments and send receipts or service notices.
  • To review teaching quality and answer support or safety questions.
  • To protect the account against fraud or abuse.

4. Lesson recordings

A 25-minute live lesson may be recorded. Authorized quality-assurance staff in the Philippines may review it. The recording is deleted after 7 days. If that class has an open safety or dispute ticket, the recording stays until the ticket is closed, and is then deleted. Teachers do not keep student recordings on personal devices.

5. Where data is processed

RemoEd PH processes personal information in the Philippines. The service uses these processors:

  • MongoDB Atlas, for the account database.
  • PayMongo, for checkout.
  • Google, for optional sign-in and for site analytics.
  • Hostinger, for email from support@remoedph.com.
  • Meta, for the advertising pixel. On the assessment page it loads after the parent accepts cookies. The pixel receives event names only. It does not receive a child’s name, age, or email. It does not load inside a signed-in student page or a live classroom.

We do not sell personal information.

6. Your rights

A parent may ask RemoEd PH to:

  • give access to the personal information we hold,
  • correct information that is wrong,
  • delete the child’s information,
  • object to a use of that information,
  • receive a portable copy of the information we can provide.

Send the request from the parent email on the account to privacy@remoedph.com, or use Request child data deletion from the student profile. We may keep payment, fraud, or open safety records when the law requires it. Those records are not used for marketing.

7. Cookies

The site stores a login session, a language choice, and a cookie choice. On the assessment page, the Meta pixel loads after the parent chooses Accept on the cookie banner. Choosing Reject leaves that pixel unloaded.

8. Updates

We may update this policy. The date at the top changes when we do. A new privacy version asks the parent to accept again before booking or paying.

9. Contact

Data Protection Officer: privacy@remoedph.com. RemoEd PH, Cabanatuan City, Nueva Ecija, Philippines.

Teachers and contractors have a separate privacy acknowledgment inside the teacher portal. This page is for parents and students only.
