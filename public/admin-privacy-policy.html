Admin & Back-Office Privacy Policy

REMOED ADMIN & BACK-OFFICE PRIVACY POLICY

Platform: RemoEd (Filipino ESL & Education Platform)

Effective Date: ________________________

RemoEd is committed to maintaining robust privacy standards for administrative personnel while safeguarding all operational, student, and teacher data processed on our platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Admin Profile Data: Full legal name, contact details, government identification, payment credentials, and assigned role scope.
  • System Audit Logs: IP addresses, login timestamps, administrative actions performed in the backend, record modification entries, and system audit trails.

2. How Administrative Information is Used

  • Managing monthly retainer payouts, payroll processing, and task management.
  • Securing administrative access controls and preventing unauthorized system breaches.
  • Auditing backend activity to ensure compliance with company policies, data governance, and ethical standards.

3. Administrative Data Handling & Child Protection

  • Privileged Access Responsibility: Staff members handling child data, parent communications, or teacher records must comply with strict non-disclosure guidelines.
  • Zero Unauthorized Dissemination: Admins are strictly barred from downloading, screenshotting, exporting, or sharing sensitive student records, personal images, or platform metrics to personal devices or outside networks.

4. Security Standards & Account Accountability

  • Administrative accounts utilize encrypted credentials, role-based access restrictions, and session tracking.
  • Privileged access levels are audited regularly and revoked immediately upon contract termination or role transition.

5. Consent & Electronic Execution

By selecting "I Accept" or accessing the RemoEd administrative portal, you consent to the data collection, auditing, and privacy terms set forth in this Privacy Policy.
