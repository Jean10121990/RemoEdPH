REMOED TEACHER TERMS OF SERVICE AND INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

Company: RemoEd (Filipino ESL & Online Education Startup)

Effective Date: ________________________

By clicking "I Accept", signing electronically, or printing and executing this document, you (“Tutor” or “Teacher”) agree to comply with all terms outlined below, alongside the RemoEd Culture & Conduct Manual and Teacher Onboarding Guidelines.

1. Nature of Relationship (Freelance Status)

  • Independent Contractor Status: The Tutor is engaged solely as an independent contractor/freelance educator. This agreement does not create an employer-employee relationship.
  • Benefits Exclusion: Freelance tutors are not eligible for company-provided benefits (including, but not limited to, SSS, Pag-IBIG, PhilHealth, or HMO coverage) unless officially transitioned to regular employment status by RemoEd management.
  • Tax Responsibilities: The Tutor is independently responsible for filing, reporting, and paying all applicable local and national taxes (including BIR filings) derived from earnings on the platform.

2. Compensation & Payout Schedule

  • Base Calculation: Salary is calculated based on the Tutor’s agreed hourly teaching rate multiplied by total actual teaching hours completed.
  • Payout Schedule: Earnings are distributed on a bi-monthly basis on the 15th and 30th/31st (or final day) of each month.
  • Payout Adjustments: Payments are subject to adjustments, referral incentives, or penalty deductions as defined in Annex A.

3. Christian Niche, Values & Code of Conduct

  • Mission & Standards: As a Christian-niche platform, RemoEd holds educators to high standards of moral integrity, grace, professionalism, and care.
  • Biblical Model of Gender & Harmony: All staff and tutors uphold the biblical distinction of gender (male and female) and agree to work harmoniously, treating fellow teachers, administrators, students, and parents with dignity and respect.
  • Academic Integrity: Tutors must input all lesson details, student records, and performance assessments honestly, accurately, and with utmost integrity.

4. Identity Protection & Security

  • Platform Nickname: For safety and personal privacy, all tutors will be assigned or must select an approved nickname for public platform profiles and student interactions. Tutors must not reveal full legal names or personal contact details to students or parents.

5. Intellectual Property & Confidentiality

  • Proprietary Materials: All curriculum materials, lesson slides, visual assets, software tools, and software resources (RemoEdPH / RemoEd) are exclusive intellectual property.
  • Strict Non-Distribution: Tutors are prohibited from copying, sharing, downloading, or distributing RemoEd materials to third parties.
  • Student Privacy: Tutors must maintain strict confidentiality regarding student names, personal details, photos, or performance records.
  • Non-Disclosure Agreement: Execution of this agreement serves as binding acceptance of the Non-Disclosure Covenant (Annex C).

6. Company Culture, Community Activities & Performance Incentives

  • Community Stewardship: In alignment with RemoEd’s mission of environmental and social stewardship, tutors are expected to actively cooperate and participate in company-sponsored activities, including tree-planting initiatives, feeding programs, and outreach efforts.
  • Referral Incentives: Tutors receive bonus incentives for referring new students who successfully enroll in RemoEd courses.
  • Badges & Awards: Tutors earn performance badges, awards, and monetary bonuses by meeting performance indicators (punctuality, high rating, attendance).
  • Path to Regularization: Opportunity for regular employment, added benefits, and long-term career growth is evaluated based on company expansion, performance indicators, and alignment with RemoEd culture.

7. Proper Use of Systems & Company Property

Tutors must operate all platform features, breakout tools, classroom technologies, and company equipment responsibly and professionally. Misuse or deliberate misuse of system access will lead to immediate contract termination.
