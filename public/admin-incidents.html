Classroom SOS

A teacher presses Emergency SOS in the live classroom. This page lists those alerts. Observe joins that class without a camera. Pause and End apply to that room only. An empty list means no teacher has sent an SOS.

OpenTeacher just sent the SOS. No admin action yet. The teacher can withdraw it for 15 seconds.
WithdrawnTeacher cancelled it within 15 seconds. The alert stays on record. The class is not ended.
ObservingAn admin joined to watch, with no camera. The student waiting state does not treat them as the teacher.
PausedThe call in that room is paused. Microphones and cameras stop.
EndedThe class was ended. A technical failure can restore one lesson credit. Other reasons do not change credits.
ResolvedThe alert is closed after it was handled.
AllShows every status together.
