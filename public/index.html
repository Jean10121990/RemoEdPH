Questions parents ask

How long is a class?

Live RemoEd classes are 25 minutes, designed for children ages 3–6.

Is it too much screen time for a young child?

Classes are short, 25 minutes with a live teacher, and parents are welcome to sit in and take part.

Can we pick a time that fits our family?

Yes. You choose and book class times that fit your family's routine after you pick a plan.

What do we need to join a class?

Details coming soon. Contact support@remoedph.com

What if we need to reschedule or cancel a class?

Details coming soon. Contact support@remoedph.com

How do credits and validity work?

Each plan has a validity window (RemoSpark 3 months, RemoSteady 6, RemoScholar 12, RemoSummit 24). Unused lesson credits expire when that date passes. Classes you have already booked are not cancelled.

About RemoEd

Who we are

RemoEd is a Filipino ESL start-up empowering kids across Asia with an accessible, God-centered online education that fosters English proficiency, moral integrity, and environmental stewardship—investing our grace-given success back into local communities.

Our principles emphasize a Christian worldview, honoring each person, and uphold the biblical distinction of gender in all conduct.
