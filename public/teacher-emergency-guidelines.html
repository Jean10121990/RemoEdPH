Use Emergency SOS in the live classroom, beside Settings. Students do not see that button. Confirm sends the alert to RemoEd admins. You can withdraw it for 15 seconds.
Tier 1

Technical failure

Power or internet has failed. Press SOS, tell the student to wait, and stay if you can. An admin may end the class and restore the lesson.

Tier 2

Unaccompanied child under 6

Press SOS and ask for a parent. Do not continue the lesson alone with a child under 6.

Tier 3

Unauthorized adult or safety

A behavioral issue, an unauthorized adult, or distress and inappropriate content. Press SOS, stop the activity, and stay in the room until an admin joins or ends it. Do not argue with the adult.

What each SOS status means

Open
You just sent the alert. Admins have not acted yet. You can withdraw it for 15 seconds.
Withdrawn
You cancelled the SOS in those 15 seconds. Admins still see it, marked withdrawn. The class is not ended.
Observing
An admin joined the class to watch. They have no camera, so the student does not see them as the teacher.
Paused
An admin paused the call. Microphones and cameras in that room stop until the class continues or ends.
Ended
An admin ended the class. A technical failure can restore the lesson. Other reasons do not change credits.
Resolved
An admin closed the alert after it was handled.
